## Problem

The previous seam-prep operation was performed by hand. This operation took an entire shift and required a skilled technician to monitor the process constantly. 

## What I did

* Developed a modular CNC seam-cutting fixture
* Enabled qualification testing ahead of new production line
* Applied GD\&T for repeatable alignment to the CNC head. 

## Outcome

* Cut anticipated prep time per assembly from 6 hours to 30 minutes.
* Cut-to-cut variation dropped, and operator error eliminated, reducing non-conformances.
