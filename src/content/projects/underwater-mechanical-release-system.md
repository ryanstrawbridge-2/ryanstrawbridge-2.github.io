## Problem

* Handle release loads up to 90 kg.
* Rated for 300m depth and underwater environments.
* Resettable without opening the housing
* Target production cost under $400

## What I did (so far…)

* Leading the mechanical release design
* Surveyed the patent and commercial landscape across motor-driven, burn-wire, corrodible-pin, and magnetic release families.
* Evaluated and ruled out magnetic coupling through the pressure vessel wall through analytical modeling and experimental testing

## Outcome

* Decomposed the release system into internal actuation and external mechanism subsystems linked by a single rotary shaft
